# Project Introduction :
This project aims to uncover valuable business insights for Atliq Hardware, a leading computer peripherals company, using SQL-based ad-hoc analysis. Developed as part of the [Codebasics Resume Project Challenge](https://codebasics.io/challenges/codebasics-resume-project-challenge/7), it demonstrates how data-driven insights can support strategic decision-making and address real-world business challenges.
# Overview

AtliQ Hardwares is a leading computer hardware producer based in India, with a growing presence in international markets. As the business expands, management needs timely and meaningful insights to make informed business decisions.

This project simulates a real-world business scenario in which I take on the role of a Junior Data Analyst, working on ad-hoc business requests from the management team.

# Business Problem

AtliQ Hardwares' management identified a gap in its ability to access actionable insights quickly. Without sufficient analytical insights, making effective and data-driven business decisions becomes challenging.

To address this challenge, the company decided to strengthen its data analytics team by hiring junior data analysts with strong technical and communication skills.

# Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyse the company's business data using SQL and answer 10 ad-hoc business requests.

The analysis aims to:
- Extract meaningful insights from the available business data.
- Identify trends and patterns that support business decisions.
- Translate business questions into SQL queries.
- Present analytical findings in a clear and structured format for top-level management.

# Dataset & Resources

The project uses the resources provided as part of the SQL challenge:

- **Dataset:** Business data required for analysis.
- **Metadata:** Information about tables, columns, and data relationships.
- **Ad-hoc Requests:** A document containing 10 business questions.

# Approach & Methodology

The project follows a structured data analysis workflow:

**Step 1 – Understand the Business Requirements**  
Reviewed the 10 ad-hoc requests to understand the business questions, expected outputs, and insights required by management.

**Step 2 – Explore the Dataset**  
Examined the available tables, columns, metadata, and relationships to understand the database structure and identify relevant information.

**Step 3 – Develop SQL Queries**  
Translated each business request into SQL queries using appropriate techniques such as joins, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs, and window functions wherever required.

**Step 4 – Analyse the Results**  
Executed the queries and examined the outputs to identify key trends, patterns, comparisons, and business insights.

**Step 5 – Present Business Insights**  
Converted the SQL findings into meaningful business takeaways, focusing on what the results indicate and how they can support management decisions.

**Step 6 – Prepare the Final Presentation**  
Designed a presentation for top-level management to communicate the analysis, key findings, and actionable insights in a clear and concise manner.

# Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI**: For creating interactive dashboards and visualizing data insights.

# Ad-hoc Business Requests

This project addresses 10 ad-hoc business requests to derive insights into sales, products, customers, markets, and overall business performance.

1.  Identify the APAC markets where AtliQ Exclusive operates.
2.  Calculate the percentage increase in unique products from 2020 to 2021.
3.  Compare unique product counts across different product segments.
4.  Identify the segment with the highest product growth in 2021.
5.  Identify products with the highest and lowest manufacturing costs.
6.  Identify the top 5 customers receiving the highest average discounts in the Indian market.
7.  Track monthly gross sales for AtliQ Exclusive to identify high- and low-performing months.
8.  Identify the quarter with the highest sold quantity in 2020.
9.  Identify the top-performing sales channel based on gross sales and contribution percentage.
10. Identify the top 3 best-selling products within each division based on sales quantity in 2021.

# Key Insights & Findings

The SQL analysis revealed several important business insights that can support better decision-making:

1. **Market Analysis:** Identified the APAC markets where AtliQ Exclusive operates, providing an overview of its regional presence.

2. **Product Growth:** Observed a **36.33% increase** in unique products in 2021 compared to 2020, highlighting the company's expanding product portfolio.

3. **Product Distribution:** Notebooks, Accessories, and Peripherals emerged as the segments with the highest product variety.

4. **Segment Growth:** Accessories and Notebooks recorded the highest growth in unique products during 2021.

5. **Manufacturing Cost Analysis:** Identified AQ Home Alin 1 Gen 2 Desktop as the highest-cost product and AQ Master Wired Mouse as the lowest-cost product, offering insights into production costs.

6. **Discount Analysis:** Flipkart recorded the highest average pre-invoice discount, highlighting differences in customer discount strategies.

7. **Monthly Sales Trends:** November recorded the highest gross sales, while March had the lowest, providing useful insights for seasonal sales planning.

8. **Quarterly Performance:** Q1 recorded the highest total sold quantity in 2020, highlighting the importance of effective inventory planning.

9. **Sales Channel Performance:** Retailers contributed **73.22% of total gross sales** in 2021, demonstrating their significant role in revenue generation.

10. **Top-Selling Products:** Pen drives and gaming accessories emerged among the top-selling products across divisions, providing insights into product demand and sales performance.
